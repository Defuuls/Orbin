About

Orbin browses 4chan (vichan/4chan-compatible) and BBW Chan (LynxChan) today, and its provider architecture means new engines can be added by implementing a single interface — no app changes required. It targets Android 12+, is a read-only browsing client (no posting), and ships regular signed releases.

Subscribed feed

Follow boards and read their most active threads in one feed, grouped by board. Phones show full-width cards; tablets and foldables get adjustable columns. Pull down to refresh.

Catalogs and two-pane reading

Board catalogs are an adaptive grid of every thread. On a wide screen the catalog stays beside the open thread.

Thread reader

Greentext, quote links with previews, backlinks, spoilers, collapsible posts, relative times, a Files view of every attachment, and a remembered reading position. Watch a thread to be told about new replies.

Save a whole thread

Save page writes a thread as a .zip: an index.html that opens in any browser, its stylesheet, and every image, video and thumbnail. Download all saves just the files.

Media

Images and video in the app, with zoom, swipe between files, and video controls. Each board has a media wall of its files, with an optional deep scan that finds files in replies too.

Content filter

An always-on filter removes gore, shock and abuse from every board, feed and search. Cover violent media (on by default) hides pictures in posts about violence until you open them.

Looks and comfort

Material 3 with dynamic colour on Android, light, dark and true-black modes, system font scaling, and screen-reader support. A What’s new card after each update says what changed.

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or ads. Every request is HTTPS with encrypted DNS. Android encrypts local data with SQLCipher and a Keystore-backed DataStore; iOS stores SQLite and settings under Data Protection (settings are not separately encrypted). An optional app lock keeps the reader closed. See the privacy policy.

Android

Orbin for Android needs Android 12 or newer. Signed APKs (orbin-v<version>.apk) are published on GitHub Releases with SHA-256 checksums for every release.

  1. Get the APK from the latest release page and allow installs from your browser when Android asks. You can also track updates with Obtainium using this repository’s URL.
  2. Verify the download (optional but encouraged) with the checksum file attached to the release: 
    sha256sum -c orbin-<version>.apk.sha256
  3. Updates are one-way. Once a version has run on your device, older versions will not start on it. Uninstalling and reinstalling clears that, at the cost of your local data.
  4. Stay up to date. Orbin checks for new versions inside the app (Settings → Check for updates, or automatically on launch when “Tell me about new releases” is enabled — on by default), or watch the releases page.

iOS

Orbin for iPhone and iPad shares its sites, filters, settings and screens with Android through Kotlin Multiplatform, with native video, the share sheet, Files and Photos, and Apple Pencil support on iPad. It needs iOS 16 or newer.

  1. It’s in beta on TestFlight. Builds go to TestFlight testers with each release; Orbin isn’t on the App Store.
  2. Same releases as Android. The iOS build carries the Android release’s version and changelog, currently {{RELEASE_NUMBER}} — {{RELEASE_CODENAME}}.
  3. Build it yourself. The iOS guide covers building in Xcode and setting up your own TestFlight.

Privacy & security

What Orbin keeps, and how, depends on the platform; the privacy policy is the source of truth.

Stored on the device

History, bookmarks, downloads and searches stay on the device. Android encrypts them with SQLCipher and a Keystore-backed DataStore. iOS uses SQLite and a DataStore file under Data Protection; the settings file is not separately encrypted.

Locked down

An optional app lock — strong biometrics or the device credential on Android, Face ID, Touch ID or the passcode on iOS — with locked content kept out of the app switcher.

Quiet on the wire

HTTPS only, DNS-over-HTTPS (Cloudflare, Google, OpenDNS or NextDNS), no analytics, and no cloud backup of local data.

Development

Orbin is open source under the AGPLv3 license, built as a strict multi-module Clean Architecture Kotlin Multiplatform project: Android and iOS share the models, sites, storage and screens. Contributions are welcome.

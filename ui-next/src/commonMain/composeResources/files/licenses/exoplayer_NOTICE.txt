The six 32dp transport vectors in ReaderMediaControls.kt use the unmodified
path data from ExoPlayer 2.9.0's Apache-licensed exo_icon_play, pause, previous,
next, rewind and fastforward resources, also distributed by Clover v3.0.2.
https://github.com/google/ExoPlayer/tree/r2.9.0/library/ui/src/main/res/drawable-anydpi-v21

Layout measurements were independently implemented in Compose from the
matching Clover 3.0.2 XML and source bindings. No Clover application source,
branding, logos or proprietary assets are included.
